Privacy Policy

How the game handles information on Android, including optional purchases and optional rewarded ads.

Last updated: 10 November 2026
Contact: jonee0001@gmail.com

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes information handling by Pocket Slime Ranch ("the game", "we", "us"), an Android mobile game distributed through Google Play. If you have questions, contact us using the email address above.

2. Your game save stays on your device

The game does not require an account, login, or player profile on a game-operated server. Gameplay information such as collected slimes, progression, currencies (including Gems), items, settings and achievements is stored on your device so the game can remember your progress. The game does not provide cloud saves, player-to-player chat, or public leaderboards, and we do not operate a server that receives your save.

If you make a purchase, the save also keeps a short purchase record (the product and Google Play's order reference) so the game can deliver each purchase exactly once. It does not contain payment details.

Android or your device's backup service may back up app data if device backup is enabled; those backups are controlled by your device and backup provider.

3. Optional in-app purchases (Google Play)

The game is fully playable without paying. Optional purchases (Gem packs, Ad-Free Rewards, the Keeper's Starter Bundle and the Celestial Sanctuary ranch theme) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details; Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.

One-time purchases are linked to your Google Play account. When the game connects to Google Play it checks them again, so ownership of Ad-Free Rewards and Celestial Sanctuary returns after a reinstall. If Google Play confirms that you own the Keeper's Starter Bundle, its two permanent incubator slots are restored on the new save as well. If there is no room for a slot yet, the game remembers it on your device and adds it later, the next time the game starts after room becomes available (for example, after you unlock another biome).

Gems and other in-game items are part of your local save: they are lost if you clear the app's data or uninstall the game without a device backup. Gems that come with a one-time purchase, such as the Starter Bundle's Gems, are given only once and are not given again after a reinstall.

The game uses Unity's In-App Purchasing library to talk to Google Play. Each time you make a purchase, this library sends a purchase report to Unity, which collects it on our behalf for transaction and revenue reporting. The report is part of how the library works, so it is sent whatever your privacy choices. It includes the product, its price and currency, the Google Play order details and purchase receipt, the app and its version, basic device information (such as model, operating system, language and storage size), a Unity installation identifier and a session identifier. We may see these purchases in Unity's reporting tools. The game tells Unity that it does not consent to Unity using this information for analytics or advertising purposes; with that setting, Unity's purchasing library does not collect your advertising ID. Unity's handling is described in Unity's Privacy Policy.

4. Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus. It never shows ads you did not ask for, and nothing in the game requires watching an ad. Ad-Free Rewards gives the same bonuses without ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Each time the game starts, it asks Google's consent service whether a consent message is needed where you live; this contacts Google even if you never watch an ad. When ads are allowed, the ads SDK starts and loads an ad in the background so that it is ready if you choose to watch one (if you own Ad-Free Rewards, no ads are loaded). In doing so, Google may collect and process information such as your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and advertising identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), app interactions, and diagnostic information, to show, measure and protect ads. Some of this is processed even for non-personalised ads, for example to limit how often an ad is shown and to prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game uses Google's consent message to ask for your choices before any ad is requested. In US states with consumer privacy laws, you can opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising.

Where Google's consent tools require it, you can review or change your choices at any time in Settings › Credits & Legal › Privacy Choices. Refusing personalised ads does not limit the game; you may still be offered non-personalised ads where Google permits them.

Your advertising choices apply to advertising. The game does not use them as permission for analytics, and it does not include an analytics service.

6. How information is used and shared

Locally stored gameplay data is used to run the game and keep your progress. We do not sell personal information, and we do not send your game save to advertisers. Information processed by Google (Play, AdMob, consent) or Unity is handled under their policies linked above.

If you email us about support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, and use it only to reply and to fix problems.

7. Your choices and requests to providers

We do not keep your game save or this information on a server of our own. Unity collects the purchase reports described above on our behalf: to ask for access to them or their deletion, contact us and we will pass your request to Unity, or use the privacy request options in Unity's Privacy Policy. Google processes ad, consent and Google Play purchase information under its own policies: use your Google Account and ad settings, and the Android setting that resets or deletes your advertising ID. We will help you find the right place to send any request.

8. Storage, retention and deletion

You can delete the local save by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Google Play keeps your purchase history under its own policy, and advertising and consent data are kept by Google under its policy; we cannot delete information held by those providers for you. A device backup, if enabled, is kept by its backup provider.

We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you. You may ask us to delete them by contacting jonee0001@gmail.com.

9. Children and intended audience

Pocket Slime Ranch is designed for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. The game does not ask for your age or invite you to create a public profile, and it is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians with a concern can contact us using the email address above.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the game's features or data practices change, publish the new version at this webpage, and change the last-updated date above.
