Privacy Policy

How the game handles information in its initial Android closed-testing release.

Last updated: 11 October 2026
Contact: jonee0001@gmail.com

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes information handling by Pocket Slime Ranch ("the game", "we", "us"), an Android mobile game. If you have questions, contact us using the email address above. This policy describes the initial closed-testing release; we will update it if the game's features or data practices change.

2. Accounts and gameplay information

The game does not require an account, login, or player profile on a game-operated server. Gameplay information such as collected slimes, progression, currencies, settings, and game achievements is stored on your device so the game can remember your progress. The game does not currently provide cloud synchronisation, player-to-player chat, or public leaderboards.

We do not operate a server that receives your saved slime collection or game progression. Android or the device's backup service may independently back up application data if device backup is enabled; the availability and retention of those backups depend on your device and backup provider's settings.

3. Third-party software and technical information

This initial release does not offer active in-app purchases or rewarded video advertisements. Although those features are disabled in the game's own code, the build may still contain software libraries from Google and Unity, including components associated with Google Play services, Google Mobile Ads, and Google Play Billing.

Depending on how bundled third-party components operate, providers may process technical information such as IP address, device or application identifiers, app interactions, and performance or diagnostic data. Such processing, if it occurs, is governed by the applicable provider's practices. We do not use a game-operated account system or analytics service to associate that information with your slime collection.

For information about providers' privacy practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and Unity's Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used and shared

Locally stored gameplay data is used to provide game functionality and preserve your progress. We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally transmit your gameplay save to advertisers or operate a cloud database of player saves. Any technical information independently processed by third-party software is subject to the providers' policies described above.

If you voluntarily email us about support or testing, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond to your enquiry, troubleshoot the game, and improve it.

5. Storage, security, retention and deletion

Gameplay data is stored in the app's local storage, with access controlled by your device's operating system. You can remove the local copy by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. A device backup, if enabled, may continue to be retained by its backup provider in accordance with its own policy.

We keep voluntary support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for support and follow-up. You may request deletion of correspondence that we retain by contacting jonee0001@gmail.com.

Where third-party services handle information, their own security and retention practices apply. We cannot delete backups or information retained by third-party service providers on your behalf.

6. Children and intended audience

Pocket Slime Ranch is primarily designed for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. The game does not ask players for their age or invite them to create a public profile. It is not intentionally designed to solicit personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians with a concern can contact us using the email address above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes, particularly before adding purchases, advertisements, accounts, analytics or online services. We will publish the revised policy at this webpage and change the last-updated date above. Any additional notices or consent required by law will be provided when applicable.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, data handling, or support can be sent to jonee0001@gmail.com.
